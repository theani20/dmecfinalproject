### Keyword Strategy Explanation

The anchor term for this page is **"smart indoor garden kit"** — it carries strong transactional intent, moderate competition, and directly describes KindleRoot's core product. The secondary keywords like "grow herbs at home" and "indoor plants for beginners" capture a broader audience at the top of the funnel and are woven into educational sections (Value Proposition, How It Works) to build topical authority. The long-tail keywords target question-based queries that Indian urban consumers actually search — phrases like "how to grow herbs indoors in India" and "how to start a kitchen garden in a small apartment" — which competitors largely ignore in their on-page content, giving KindleRoot a first-mover advantage in featured snippet and People Also Ask positions. Competitor analysis revealed that most rival brands optimise for generic terms like "planters online" and "indoor plants" but miss the "smart" and "subscription" modifiers entirely, leaving a clear gap for KindleRoot to own the AI-assisted, subscription gardening niche.

## B3. BACKLINK QUALITY CRITERIA

| Criterion | Minimum Acceptable | Ideal Target |
|---|---|---|
| Domain Authority (DA/DR) | DA 20+ | DA 45+ |
| Topical Relevance | Broadly related (home, lifestyle) | Directly related (gardening, smart home, wellness, food) |
| Monthly Traffic of Linking Page | 500+ visits/month | 5,000+ visits/month |
| Link Type (follow/nofollow) | Nofollow acceptable from DA 60+ sites for referral traffic | Do-follow from DA 30–70 sites |
| Anchor Text Type | Branded or generic acceptable | Mix: 40% branded, 30% keyword-rich, 20% generic, 10% naked URL |
| Red Flags to Always Avoid | PBN networks, link farms, sitewide footer links, irrelevant foreign-language domains, pages with 100+ outbound links, any site penalised by Google, paid link schemes without nofollow | — |

## B5. TOOLS & TRACKING

**Discovery & Prospecting:**
- Ahrefs (backlink analysis, broken link finding, competitor gap analysis)
- Screaming Frog SEO Spider (site crawling and broken link detection)
- BuzzSumo (finding shareable content ideas and influencer identification)

**Outreach & Follow-up:**
- Pitchbox (automated outreach sequences with personalisation)
- Hunter.io (finding journalist and editor email addresses)
- GMass or Woodpecker (email outreach tracking and follow-ups)

**Monitoring & Reporting:**
- Google Search Console (tracking referring domains, impressions, and click-through rates)
- Ahrefs Alerts (new and lost backlink notifications)
- SEMrush Position Tracking (monitoring keyword ranking changes tied to link-building efforts)

**Monthly tracking metrics to review:**
- Total new referring domains acquired this month
- Domain Authority / Domain Rating change (month-over-month)
- Organic traffic growth to target landing pages (via Google Analytics)
- Keyword ranking movement for primary and long-tail keywords (via SEMrush/Ahrefs)
- Referral traffic volume and conversion rate from backlink sources (via Google Analytics UTM tracking)
